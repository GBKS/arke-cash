# Test 3: Make your first payments

Now let's try paying a Lightning invoice. Arké works with the Lightning network, so you can pay any Lightning invoice straight from your payments balance. You'll send bitcoin over Ark to other Arké users in the next test.

##### 3.1: Get a Lightning invoice

Create a small invoice in another Lightning wallet you own, or find something small to buy from a shop that accepts Lightning. Copy the invoice.

##### 3.2: Pay it

On the **Send** screen, tap the paste button on the right, above the tabs, and allow the paste when iOS asks. Check the amount and tap **Send**. Lightning payments should go through in a few seconds.

## On signet

On signet, you can practice both kinds of payments with test bitcoin.

##### Send bitcoin back to Faucetto Signetto

Tap the paper airplane next to Faucetto Signetto, pick an amount, and tap **Send**. This sends over Ark and puts test bitcoin back in the pot for other testers.

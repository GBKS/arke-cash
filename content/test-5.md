# Test 5: Tag your transactions

Once you've made a few payments, you may want to organize them. Here's how tags work.

##### 5.1: Tag a transaction

Find the first bitcoin you received in Test 2. Tap it to see details and assign a tag of your choice to it. Feel free to create a new tag if you'd like.

##### 5.2: View your tag overview

At the top of the **Activity** screen, you can access your full list of tags. They help you stay organized and see how you spend your bitcoin.

Notice the **Balance** tag. It's a special tag that Arké applies automatically to balance maintenance, like transfers and refreshes.

##### 5.3: Filter by tag

Tapping a tag lets you filter your activity list by that tag.
